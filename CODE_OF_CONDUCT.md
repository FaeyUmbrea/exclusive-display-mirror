# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Version 2.1

Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at: security@OWNER-REPO.example (replace with a real contact email). All reports will be handled with discretion and investigated and responded to in a timely manner.

When a report is received, the maintainers will follow these steps:
1. Acknowledge receipt within 3 business days.
2. Gather relevant information and, where appropriate, contact involved parties for more details.
3. Take action based on the severity of the incident which may include: a private warning, temporary or permanent ban from the project, contacting platform administrators, or other actions as needed.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community (e.g., social media, conferences).

Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. If a maintainer is the subject of a report, the report should be escalated to the contact email above and handled by an impartial member or external advisor.

Addressing AI-generated content

- AI-generated content (code, text, or other artifacts) is subject to the same Code of Conduct as human-authored content. If AI is used to generate content that violates this Code of Conduct, the same reporting and enforcement steps apply.

Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).


Contact and escalation

If you feel you have been subject to or witnessed behavior that violates this code and the security email is not appropriate, you may also open a confidential issue addressed to the maintainers or contact an external mediator.
